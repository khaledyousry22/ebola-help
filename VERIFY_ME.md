# Contact details waiting for your check

Nothing on this list appears on the website. A phone number, address or
health facility is published only after you have confirmed it.

## How to check an entry

1. Open the **official source** link.
2. Check that the number or address on that page is exactly the same as in
   the table (every digit).
3. If it matches, tick the box and write today's date. If it does not match,
   or the page no longer exists, write what you found in "Notes".

Then tell Claude (or edit the file in GitHub) and the entry will be published
with its source link and "last verified" date.
