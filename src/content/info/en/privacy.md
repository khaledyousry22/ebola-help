## In short

You can use Ebola.help without registering or giving us any personal information. We do not use analytics or tracking tools of our own. Google and Adsterra, which show the ads on some pages, may use cookies; you can turn off personalised Google ads.

## Who is responsible

Ebola.help is run by the Ebola.help Team. You can contact us at [wrongnumber@ebola.help](mailto:wrongnumber@ebola.help).

## What is stored on your device

- **Your language.** When you choose a language, a small cookie called `ebh_locale` remembers it for one year, so the site opens in your language next time.
- **Your country.** If you choose your country in the emergency box, your choice is saved in your browser so that the right helpline is shown next time. It is not sent to us.
- **Pages for use without a connection.** To work offline, the site saves some pages on your device: the home page, the helplines, the country pages and some health pages. They are not sent to us.

You can delete all of this at any time by clearing your browser's data for ebola.help.

## Advertising (Google AdSense and Adsterra)

The home page, the news pages and the map show ads from Google AdSense. Pages with health advice and helplines never show ads.

Google and other advertising companies use cookies to show ads. These ads may be based on your previous visits to this website and to other websites. Google explains [how it uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

You can turn off personalised advertising in [Google's Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/) or at [aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/). Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for their consent before ads are personalised.

No ads are loaded when your browser asks to save data or when the connection is very slow.

The same pages can also show native ads from Adsterra: a small panel of sponsored links, marked as an advertisement. Adsterra may use cookies or similar technologies to show and count these ads; see [Adsterra's privacy policy](https://adsterra.com/privacy-policy/). Adsterra ads are not shown to visitors whose device is set to a European time zone.

## Hosting

The site is hosted by Cloudflare. To deliver pages and protect the site against attacks, Cloudflare processes technical information such as your IP address. Cloudflare gives us only overall figures, such as the number of visits per day, which do not identify anyone. See [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

## If you write to us

If you send us an e-mail, we use your message and your e-mail address only to check and correct the error you report and to reply to you. We do not share them with anyone else. Our e-mail is provided by Zoho Mail ([Zoho's privacy policy](https://www.zoho.com/privacy.html)).

## Links to other websites

We link to official sources and to news websites. When you visit them, their own privacy policies apply.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date at the top.
